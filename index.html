Why Spendium

Built for your money, not for our metrics.

The essentials for understanding your spending, without handing over your data.

Your data stays yours

Your records stay on your device. There is no Spendium server collecting your expenses and no account to create.

Works fully offline

Record expenses and review your spending without a connection. Every entry is saved on your device first.

Sync on your cloud

Optionally sync through a private app folder in your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Your other files stay out of reach.

Multi-currency, made clear

Keep expenses in their original currencies. Historical exchange rates from Frankfurter help you compare them in one view.

The details and the big picture

Explore category breakdowns, search your transactions, or step back to see spending trends over time.

Any time period

A day, a month, a year, or a range of your own. Your charts and totals follow the period you choose.

Quick entry with calculator

Enter an amount, choose a category, and carry on. The built-in calculator handles receipt totals and shared costs.

15 languages

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Czech, Turkish, Ukrainian, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese (Simplified).

Backup & restore

Export a complete JSON backup or take your data into a spreadsheet with CSV. Restore a backup when you change devices.

A different kind of finance app

Your money is personal.
It should stay that way.

No Spendium account. No advertising profiles. Your records stay on your device, or in a private file in your own cloud account. You stay in control.
